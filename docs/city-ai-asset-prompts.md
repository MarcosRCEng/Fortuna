# Prompts de assets IA da Cidade Fortuna

Use este documento para manter consistencia visual em novas rodadas de geracao dos predios.

## Direcao geral

```txt
2D isometric educational city building asset, clean serious friendly style, long-term wealth building metaphor, no gambling, no casino, no slot machine, no roulette, no poker chips, no loot box, no aggressive neon, no real logos, no real stock tickers, no text, transparent background, centered object, soft shadow, polished game asset, 1024x1024 PNG.
```

## Estagios

```txt
Stage 1 Fundacao: modest foundation, small structure, early construction, simple base, restrained details.
Stage 2 Crescimento: larger active building, more volume, visible expansion, refined but not final.
Stage 3 Maturidade: complete mature building, consolidated, refined, elegant, stable and trustworthy.
```

## Predios

```txt
financial_hall: civic financial hall, dome or municipal finance architecture, trustworthy public-building feeling.
reserve_bank: secure reserve bank, vault-inspired facade, stable and conservative.
city_exchange: modern city exchange, glass and stone, abstract chart-like architectural motif only, no tickers.
real_estate_center: real estate center, mixed-use property building, homes and offices, landscaped base.
financial_school: financial school, academic building, welcoming learning architecture.
income_park: income park, landscaped public park with fountains and paths, growth without money symbols.
mentor_tower: mentor tower, vertical observatory or guidance tower, calm refined presence.
```

## Observacoes dos assets atuais

Os PNGs em `apps/web/public/assets/city/buildings` ja seguem a proposta visual: sao isometricos, sem texto embutido, sem sinais de cassino e com evolucao clara entre fundacao, crescimento e maturidade. A recomendacao tecnica e manter os PNGs para esta entrega e planejar WebP ou atlas depois para reduzir peso.
